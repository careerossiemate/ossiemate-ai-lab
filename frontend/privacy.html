Privacy statement

Effective upon publication following operator review. Aadhira Impex Pty Ltd operates the Ossiemate AI Lab and its associated learning agent.

Information and purposes

The public learning site provides educational material. Authenticated features use Microsoft Entra identity, user identifiers, submitted prompts, files, audio, generated content, progress records and execution metadata as needed to provide learning features, restrict access, monitor usage and troubleshoot. Microsoft Teams and Foundry may separately retain agent conversations under their service and tenant settings.

Processing and disclosure

Azure services process and may store information in Australia, the United States and other locations according to service configuration. Video generation sends prompts to fal infrastructure outside Microsoft facilities; Microsoft customer data protection terms do not cover that partner service. Use synthetic data only, never confidential or personal information. Service providers and authorised administrators may process data for operation and security.

Storage, retention and security

Learning progress, job identifiers, generated files and diagnostic records may persist in Azure storage. No automatic deletion period is promised; retention and deletion depend on service capabilities and administrator action. Access controls and managed identities reduce risk but cannot guarantee security.

Contact and your choices

Contact contact@aadhiraimpex.com.au for access, correction, deletion or privacy concerns. Requests will be assessed subject to applicable law and technical limitations. You may stop using the lab at any time.
